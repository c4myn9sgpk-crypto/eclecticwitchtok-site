06 · spellbook

📖 Spellbook

Simple, symbolic examples for intention-setting and personal reflection.

protection

Rosemary Protection Jar

Use rosemary as a symbolic reminder of boundaries, safety, and feeling grounded. Add a written intention and keep it somewhere meaningful.

moon work

Moon Intention Page

Write one thing you're releasing, one thing you're growing, and one thing you want to notice. Decorate the page with moon phases.

self-love

Rose Self-Love Sachet

Use dried rose petals as a symbol of kindness toward yourself. Add affirmations or tiny notes about qualities you appreciate.

clarity

Herbal Intention Bundle

Pair a few herbs by symbolism—like rosemary for protection and lavender for peace—and write the purpose beside them.

grounding

Crystal Grounding Ritual

Hold a grounding crystal, breathe slowly, and name five things you can see, four you can feel, and one intention for the moment.

journal

Tarot Reflection

Pull one card and journal: “what does this card make me notice?” and “what choice would support me today?”
